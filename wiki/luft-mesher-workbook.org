#+title: A worksheet for the columnar LUFT mesher
#+startup: overview

This is a deliberately provisional work page.  The geometric construction,
proofs, and history remain on the site-sum page (#BFSI2X); this page asks how
to execute that construction efficiently for streaming chunks.  It records
the present machine evidence, the representation thesis suggested by reading
the packed topology and query together, and an order of experiments.  Once the
implementation settles, the durable conclusions can move to the more general
LUFT pages and this worksheet can become historical evidence.

* The chain is the reusable fact; the neighborhood is the query
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: PWUMY7
:END:

The columnar mesher already removed the general builder, transient geometry
objects, template hashing, and packed intermediary jobs.  Its remaining large
costs mostly occur before and around those typed relations: each mesh rebuild
derives dense occupancy from a sparse chain, indexes neighboring chains into
hash tables, rediscovers vertical extent, and resolves material provenance
through canonical sites and world-scale hashes.  In database terms, the query
is no longer the main problem; it rematerializes its base tables for every
execution.

LUFT's actual value semantics suggest a narrower answer than mutable chunk
state.  A [[lisp:luft:make-chain][chain]] is a normalized immutable value whose packed sites are sorted in
chunk-major, column-major order.  An edit produces a new chain; unchanged
chunks retain the same chain object.  Derived state can therefore be memoized
by chain identity without an invalidation protocol.  Old facts become
unreachable with the old chain.

The boundary of that idea is as important as the idea itself:

- Memoize by chain alone only functions of one chain: occupancy fibers, column
  metadata, and vertical metadata.
- A material lane also depends on the immutable authored-material assignment
  snapshot.  Its key must name that source (and the material program if the
  lane stores interpreted stocks rather than source offsets); chain identity
  alone is not enough when occupancy stays fixed but authored material changes.
- Do not memoize star selection, topology relations, or finished meshes by one
  chain.  Seam stars depend on a neighborhood of chains, and tuple-keyed caches
  would make residency and lifetime harder for a stage that is already cheap.
- Preserve the missing-chunk and outside-domain restart contract.  The fast
  path can change the resolved representation without weakening the semantic
  boundary or the reference mesher.

The intended flow is consequently:

#+begin_example
immutable chain ──derive once──▶ chain facts
                                      │
neighborhood of chain facts ──────────┤
chain + authored material snapshot ──▶ material lane
                                      ▼
  occupancy window → selected stars → typed topology rows
                   → material lanes  → renderer instance streams
#+end_example

“Query engine” remains a useful execution analogy, not a request for a generic
planner or operator framework.  This is a fixed-schema, chunk-bounded program
whose inspectable relations currently make differential testing inexpensive.

* The baseline localizes both execution and reconstruction
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: BF4UNN
:END:

=make luft-mesher-profile= measures the production-shaped mountain-sanctuary
chunk and writes aggregate and stage-isolated SB-SPROF reports.  The first
default run under SBCL 2.6.6 measured 44,036 occupied cells, 9,946 selected
sites, and 156,252 output triangles:

| phase        | ms/chunk | aggregate | MiB/chunk |
|--------------+----------+-----------+-----------|
| aggregate    |   26.182 |    100.0% |     4.312 |
| materials    |    8.960 |     34.2% |     0.001 |
| planning     |    5.044 |     19.3% |     0.000 |
| projection   |    3.873 |     14.8% |     1.819 |
| halo index   |    3.778 |     14.4% |     2.083 |
| Z bounds     |    1.902 |      7.3% |     0.000 |
| occupancy    |    0.839 |      3.2% |     0.144 |
| selection    |    0.560 |      2.1% |     0.291 |

The isolated phases account for 95.3% of aggregate latency and essentially
all allocation.  Their profiles say more than the labels:

- Material resolution constructs validated face and cell sites, searches the
  authored-material hash, and sometimes binary-searches the world chain for
  foundation semantics.  The production scene stock closure ignores the face
  object it is handed, so part of this cost has no semantic consumer.
- =halo-index= rebuilds EQL cell hashes for the three low neighbors consulted
  by this fixture's Z-bounds pass.  A general star window can touch all eight
  neighboring chunks.  The important observation is not the exact count in
  this fixture, but that already packed chains are expanded into whole-cell
  hashes to answer narrow fiber probes.
- Planning still computes descriptor identity, edge state, and compact
  contributor masks even though these are finite functions of an eight-bit
  star mask.
- Projection scans twelve contributor slots for every patch row even when
  only a few mask bits are set, and allocates the final renderer ABI streams.

Selection's AVX2 gather is on nearly every selection-stage stack, but the whole
stage is only 2.1% of aggregate time.  That is simply the current division of
work, not a conclusion about where SIMD will or will not matter after the data
flow changes.

* Chain facts are narrow materialized views
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: 7CG2MB
:END:

One chain-derived record can give every neighboring query direct access to the
representation it currently rebuilds:

- 64 by 64 columns of four 64-bit occupancy words;
- a column-start table mapping each column to its first rank in chain order;
- enough vertical metadata to bound a window without scanning packed cells;
  and
- optionally other topology-only facts proved to depend on this chain alone.

A weak synchronized EQ table is one possible owner.  A derived-state table in
the streaming store is another.  The invariant matters more than the first
container: publication must be atomic, readers must see a fully initialized
record, and the key must be the immutable chain object rather than only its
chunk coordinate.  Concurrent requests may redundantly derive the same cheap
record, but they must never observe a partial one.

The occupancy field's resolution need not become a new public boundary.
=use-chunk= can still resolve a missing key to its chain; the width-one path can
then obtain that chain's fibers rather than call =%chunk-cells-table=.  Halo
materialization becomes copies and masks of four-word fibers instead of one
=gethash= per sampled Z cell.

Vertical metadata needs one experiment rather than an assumption.  A global
min/max per neighboring chain is a correct over-approximation, but a tall cell
far from the shared edge could enlarge the selected Z interval unnecessarily.
Per-column nonempty-word metadata, or exact ranges for the boundary strips,
may preserve the current narrow-halo bound more cheaply.  Measure selected
window volume as well as elapsed time before deleting the exact scan.

The packed ordering also makes the current full membership-validation scan
unnecessary.  Since chunk bits are the most significant site bits and a chain
is sorted, checking the first and last occupied sites proves that every site
belongs to the named chunk.  This is a small independent cut, but it is another
case where the existing representation should answer directly.

* Material identity follows occupied-cell rank
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: XP3UHE
:END:

The authored scene currently maps packed cell sites to material offsets with a
world-scale hash.  The compiled material program already turns those offsets
into dense placement flags, oriented face stocks, and chamfer summaries.  The
missing bridge is a chain-indexed material field whose key also names the
authored-material assignment snapshot.

Packed chain order makes that field compact.  Within one chunk, all occupied Z
cells of an X/Y column are contiguous.  For an occupied cell, its rank is:

#+begin_example
column-start[x,y] + popcount(occupancy bits below z)
#+end_example

A =(chain, authored-material-map)= derived value can therefore hold one
unsigned 16-bit material offset per occupied cell, in chain order.  Construction
pays one authored hash lookup per cell once for that pair.  A hot face-stock
probe then performs fiber/rank arithmetic and dense material-program reads.
Foundation classification becomes an occupancy test below the cell plus one
placement flag read; because LUFT chunks divide X and Y but span Z, that cell
remains in the same chunk.  If the derived lane stores final stocks instead of
source offsets, the material program becomes another explicit key dependency.

This compiled material field belongs on the production streaming path beside
the compiled chamfer algebra.  The closure-based source-stock protocol remains
valuable for the general reference and diagnostic callers.  Package ownership
is still open: topology-only rank data belongs in LUFT, while the association
between authored offsets and a renderer material program belongs to the scene
material layer.  The implementation should preserve that dependency direction
rather than make core topology know renderer classes.

Before building the field, there is one direct cut: the internal production
resolver can accept =(cell, axis, side)= and construct an oriented face only in
the legacy one-argument adapter.  The current production closure ignores its
=face= argument.  The profile should lose =make-site=, =site-valid-p=, and
boundary-site construction from the material stage without changing one
material result.

* Static stars should name static topology rows
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: 1D77HO
:END:

The proposal of #3IKS3Y survives the broader representation review, but no
longer needs to be the first structural step.  For every star mask, the edge
state, descriptor identity, template ID, compact contributor mask, source
sample offset, and ambient flag are immutable.  Compile them into flat typed
face, band, and fan row vocabularies once.  Planning then copies rows while
adding site and material-base ordinals instead of translating one finite
vocabulary into another at runtime.

Ownership divides sites into a large ordinary interior and a small seam.  For
a full 65 by 65 lattice window, only sites on the two X or two Y boundary
columns need the source-cell ownership predicates.  Interior sites can append
precompiled rows directly; seam rows carry their static source offsets and pay
the half-open chunk-bound checks.  This is a regular iteration surface on which
bulk copies or SIMD may eventually be natural, but the representation should
be justified first by deleting translation work.

Keep the typed relations while they remain useful evidence.  Planning and
projection could eventually fuse into template histograms and direct bucket
writes, but that would remove an inspectable differential boundary.  Fuse only
if row appends become a measured dominant cost after the larger representation
changes.

Projection has one smaller direct representation cut: iterate the set bits of
each compact contributor mask instead of testing all twelve slots.  Its 1.8
MiB of stream words is mostly final renderer output, not automatically waste;
pool it only if ownership inspection shows that it is copied or discarded
rather than handed onward as the ABI value.

* The contracts which optimization must not hide
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: L38W75
:END:

- Chains remain normalized immutable values; identity reuse is an optimization
  witness, not a substitute for chain equality.
- Cached facts depend on exactly one chain unless their key names every other
  dependency.  Material assignments are independent of occupancy, while seam
  stars and meshes are neighborhood results.
- Missing chunks and outside-domain occupancy retain explicit caller policy.
- Half-open source-cell ownership emits each seam primitive exactly once,
  including virtual owners at the world edge.
- Material resolution preserves occupied source cell, oriented axis and side,
  foundation semantics, and the closed chamfer algebra.
- Final face, band, and fan streams retain their template grouping, draw order,
  stocks, ambient values, singular-star count, and exact triangle census.
- The surface-proportional mesher remains the semantic oracle until the new
  path is stable.  The packed-job batch can be reconsidered later; removing a
  second oracle is not part of the performance work itself.

* A cohort, not one chunk, measures reuse
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: 5BAI3B
:END:

The stage profiler remains the right microscope for one query.  Chain-derived
state additionally needs a production-shaped cohort benchmark with three
named regimes:

1. /cold cohort/: no chain facts or material fields exist;
2. /warm cohort/: mesh the same resident chains again; and
3. /one edit/: replace one chain and remesh its affected owners while all
   unchanged neighbor chains retain identity.

Record total cohort latency and allocation, per-chunk distributions, derived
record builds and hits, selected-site count, relation rows, and output census.
The memo proposal is falsified if warm cohorts do not remove halo/index/Z work,
if cache residency is disproportionate to resident chain data, or if vertical
over-approximation materially enlarges selection.  The material-field proposal
is falsified if its cold construction plus retained memory does not buy back
repeated authored lookup across real remeshes.

Every step keeps the exhaustive 256-star differential, real seam and
missing-residency cases, and material-sensitive mountain-sanctuary comparison
against the reference.  Performance evidence never substitutes for those
semantic checks.

* DONE Stop constructing ignored face sites
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: XCNPYI
:END:

/Intent./ Let the production stock resolver consume the source cell, axis, and
side it actually uses; construct an oriented face lazily only for the legacy
one-argument stock callback.

/Evidence./ Material resolution is 34.2% of aggregate time.  Its flat profile
contains the complete checked-site construction chain, while
[[lisp:luft.render::make-scene-face-stock-function][the production closure]] ignores =face=.

/Done when./ All material and full-mesh differentials remain exact, the
material-stage report no longer attributes samples to constructing the ignored
face, and ordinary aggregate timing records the effect.

/Result./ The resolver protocol is now =(cell, axis, side)=;
[[lisp:luft::%make-face-stock-resolver][the legacy adapter]] is the one place that still constructs a checked
boundary-face site.  On the same mountain-sanctuary fixture the material
stage fell from 8.960 to 5.143 ms/chunk and the aggregate from 26.182 to
21.847 ms/chunk; the material flat profile retains only a 1.3% residual of
=site-valid-p= from foundation occupancy probes, with =gethash=, the
world-chain binary search, and =chain-cell-occupancy-bit= now dominating --
exactly the cost named by the material-field experiment below.  The full
LUFT differential suite and a query-versus-reference triangle-record census
over all four resident production chunks remained exact.

* DONE Reuse chain-local occupancy facts
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: IS4S0L
:END:

/Intent./ Derive fibers, column starts, and adequate vertical metadata once per
immutable chain; let width-one neighbor resolutions consume those facts
without whole-chain cell hashes.

/Evidence./ Occupancy, halo indexing, and Z-bounds currently cost about 6.5 ms
and 2.2 MiB per build before selection begins.  Unchanged streaming neighbors
retain EQ-identical chains across remeshes.

/Done when./ Cold, warm, and one-edit cohort measurements expose the expected
reuse; no narrow-halo selection explodes; concurrent publication is complete;
and seam, missing-chunk, and outside-domain differentials remain exact.

/Result./ [[lisp:luft::%chain-chunk-facts][A weak-keyed table]] now publishes one complete derived record per
immutable single-chunk chain: occupancy fibers in field layout, chain-order
column starts, and exact Z ranges for the whole chunk, the two high strips,
and the corner column.  The owner field is a read-only wrap of those fibers;
halo bit probes and window-column fills read fibers directly; star Z-bounds
read the strip ranges, which are exact rather than over-approximated, so
selection did not grow by a single site.  Warm occupancy, halo indexing,
and Z-bounds now cost under a microsecond each (down from 0.850, 4.064, and
1.840 ms); the aggregate fell from 21.847 to 16.223 ms/chunk and allocation
from 4.3 to 2.1 MiB/chunk, with the cold derive costing 1.472 ms per
owner-plus-halo neighborhood.  The new =make luft-mesher-cohort= benchmark
confirms the lifecycle: the cold cohort builds exactly one record per
resident chain, five warm cohorts build nothing while reproducing the cold
census exactly, and one cell edit rebuilds exactly the one replaced chain.
Residency is about 144 KiB per resident chain (128 KiB fibers, 16 KiB
column starts) held weakly by chain identity.  Seam, missing-chunk, and
outside-domain differentials and the full LUFT suite remained exact, as did
the query-versus-reference triangle census over all four production chunks.
The column-starts lane already gives the material-field experiment below its
chain-order rank structure for free.

/Intent./ Resolve authored material offsets once into a chain-order lane keyed
by the chain and immutable authored-material snapshot, then make production
stock lookup a rank probe followed by dense material-program reads.

/Evidence./ Material resolution is the largest remaining stage, dominated by

answers are already represented by occupancy and the compiled material tables.

/Done when./ The production path performs no authored hash or world-chain
search per contributor, foundation semantics and all stocks match the
reference, and cohort measurements include cold construction, reuse, and
retained memory.

* IDEA Fuse relations only after they cease to explain the work
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: GPL9Z3
:END:

Static row histograms could eventually write renderer buckets directly and
remove the face/band/fan relation materialization.  The current relations are
small, typed, inspectable, and useful for differential tests.  Keep them until
profiling after chain facts, material fields, and #3IKS3Y says their appends or
reads dominate; otherwise fusion would spend clarity before it buys time.
