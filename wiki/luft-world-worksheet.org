#+title: A worksheet for the large Luft world
#+startup: overview

* Intent and non-goals
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: 59ZKO4
:END:

This is the living worksheet for turning Luft's small mountain sanctuary into
a genuinely large, demand-streamed voxel world with authored geography,
architecture, material variety, and regional voxel light.  The durable design
records in [[file:luft.org][the Luft notebook]], [[file:domains-and-bundles.org][Domains, bundles, and materialized worlds]],
[[file:frontier-traversals.org][Frontier traversals]], and [[file:block-world.org][the Luvcraft block-world notebook]] explain the
representations and prior experiments.  This page sequences the new work,
records what each experiment teaches, and names exactly one next action.
Execution threads are replaceable working sessions; they read and update this
worksheet rather than becoming the only place where a decision survives.

The intended world should feel composed before it feels generated: a river and
watershed, roads and passes, long sightlines, distinct regions, a substantial
settlement or fortress, and sparse bridges, ruins, quarries, caves, shrines,
and waystations make travel legible while still allowing wilderness, wrong
turns, and getting lost.  Deterministic procedural fields supply local terrain
and detail under that authored plan.  Walking must cause source materialization,
light solving, meshing, publication, and eviction rather than selecting meshes
from a world already held in memory.

This work does /not/ restore the mesher deleted by commits =366ffe4= and
=ae99b9d=, reintroduce mixed bevel widths or material-dependent topology,
design transparent terrain, begin with level of detail or vertical streaming,
or extract a universal voxel engine before Luft provides a second concrete
client for Luvcraft's best substrate ideas.

* Established invariants
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: 94AJDM
:END:

- Luft retains one width-one 256-star atlas.  Materials and light do not alter
  star selection, triangle ownership, bevel geometry, or the four-word active
  site stream.
- Canonical authored content names a material at each cell; occupancy is the
  projection =material != air=.  Geometry, light, meshes, and GPU resources are
  derived products with independently checkable provenance.
- World description, resident chunks, and rendered chunks are different
  things.  The source plus sparse authored overlay can rematerialize an evicted
  chunk; resident storage is not the save format.
- An absent neighbor is explicitly open sky, closed world, or unknown because
  it is nonresident.  Streaming state never silently becomes authored air,
  darkness, or open sky.
- The canvas/render owner remains the single writer of residency and GPU
  state.  Workers receive immutable captures; incarnation and dependency
  stamps make obsolete generation, light, and appearance results harmless.
- Geometry and appearance are separate products even if an early worker
  computes both.  Repainting or relighting may not change geometry bytes or
  triangle counts.
- Sun and shadow maps retain direct-light ownership.  Voxel sky light scales
  ambient illumination; RGB4 block light contributes local radiance.
- The first proof is opaque, uses one small global material vocabulary, and
  accepts dense full-height chunk lanes until measurement earns compression.
- Geography has authored macrostructure.  Noise may roughen a place but may
  not be the only reason one place differs from another.

* Current architecture and evidence
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: AMY4CI
:END:

The simplified implementation is the foundation, while deleted code is design
evidence rather than a restoration source.

- [[file:../luft/mesh.lisp][luft/mesh.lisp]] defines one =surface-mesh= as four unsigned words per
  active star.  =+MESH-BEVEL-WIDTH+= is one and the renderer metadata does not
  participate in geometry.
- [[file:../luft/mesh-query.lisp][luft/mesh-query.lisp]] selects active lattice stars from dense 64x64
  occupancy fibers and their one-cell neighborhood.
- [[file:../luft/star-table.lisp][luft/star-table.lisp]] and [[file:../luft/atlas.lisp][luft/atlas.lisp]] own the fixed geometry and
  its symmetry-derived 256 entries.  This is the place where a static
  triangle-to-incident-cell appearance selector might be proved.
- [[file:../luft/render/render.lisp][luft/render/render.lisp]] flattens only the active-site words into the
  terrain population.  Its current =streaming-scene= eagerly partitions a
  complete finite scene into an in-memory store, so only meshing and GPU
  residency are streamed.
- [[file:../luft/render/shaders.lisp][luft/render/shaders.lisp]] gives terrain fixed top, side, and bottom tones
  under ambient sky/ground, direct sun, shadow-map visibility, and fog.  It
  receives neither terrain material identity nor voxel light.
- [[file:../luft/render/materials.lisp][luft/render/materials.lisp]] and [[file:../luft/voxel-light.lisp][luft/voxel-light.lisp]] retain useful semantic
  material and RGB4 finite-solver evidence.  Current terrain does not consume
  those fields; remaining use by scene and torch paths must not be mistaken
  for a scalable regional design.
- [[file:../luft/render/render.lisp][=MAKE-HIGHLAND-SANCTUARY-SCENE=]] already sketches a 512x512 landscape
  with three sparse landmarks, but eagerly builds all sixty-four chunks and
  derives most macrostructure from value noise.  It is a content experiment,
  not the demand-world architecture.
- [[file:../domains/domains.lisp][domains/domains.lisp]] and
  [[file:../arithmetic/records/columnar.lisp][arithmetic/records/columnar.lisp]] already provide neutral finite domains,
  vocabularies, and typed columnar materializations.
- [[file:../luvcraft/world.lisp][luvcraft/world.lisp]], [[file:../luvcraft/terrain.lisp][luvcraft/terrain.lisp]], and
  [[file:../luvcraft/streaming.lisp][luvcraft/streaming.lisp]] demonstrate palette-coded chunk content,
  deterministic source materialization, sparse overlays, demand tokens,
  incarnation validation, and bounded eviction.  Their 16-cubed block-world
  classes and gameplay contracts remain Luvcraft-specific.
- [[file:../luvcraft/light.lisp][luvcraft/light.lisp]] and [[file:../luvcraft/frontier-light.lisp][luvcraft/frontier-light.lisp]] demonstrate separately
  revised columnar light fields, palette-projected propagation tables,
  explicit absent-boundary semantics, coherent regional publication, and
  compiled frontier propagation.

* Open technical questions
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: M6MATV
:END:

1. Can every triangle in every star atlas entry select one incident occupied
   cell for material and one outward empty cell for illumination by a stable,
   symmetry-preserving rule?  Junction and checkerboard stars are the decisive
   cases.
2. Is one appearance record per active star the right first ABI, containing
   the eight neighboring material codes and light values while leaving the
   active-site stream byte-identical?  Measure alignment and upload cost before
   compacting it.
3. Should the first palette be one world-wide append-only =u8= vocabulary, or
   do authored generation and editing already demonstrate a need for wider or
   chunk-local codes?  Prefer the world vocabulary until evidence says no.
4. What is the smallest immutable Luft resident-cell-chunk value that keeps
   material content canonical while making occupancy fibers and chains cheap
   derived views?
5. Can a visible radius plus one complete guard chunk settle all 15-level sky
   and RGB4 block light, including diagonal star appearance dependencies, or
   does publication require a differently shaped capture?
6. Which part of =luvcraft.world.fields= is sufficiently experiment-neutral to
   move below both games now?  Frontier program semantics appear general, but
   extraction of their execution lowering waits until Luft is a real second
   client.
7. How should authored roads, rivers, region masks, and structures compose at
   chunk boundaries without requiring one giant scene builder or making local
   noise the world plan?

* Ordered stages

:ID: N3DRNE
:END:

The stages are gates, not a backlog to execute speculatively.  An execution

writes a prose completion update, and identifies rather than begins the next

** NEXT Prove static atlas appearance selectors
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: 33UIQ6
:END:

/Intent./ Give every triangle in all 256 immutable star entries a purely static
selector for the incident occupied cell that supplies material and the
outward empty cell that supplies voxel illumination.  Keep generated geometry
and the active-site ABI unchanged.

/Evidence./ The new atlas already derives all geometry from eight-cell stars
and symmetry transforms.  A selector stored in unused atlas metadata would let
the shader interpret parallel cell fields without rebuilding faces, stocks,
material junction policy, or mixed bevel machinery.  This attractive design
must be proved over the complete atlas rather than assumed from ordinary faces.

/Done when./ Executable tests cover all atlas triangles, validate occupied and
empty selector semantics, validate transformation stability, and assert the
existing triangle census and site stream are unchanged.  The implementation
is reviewable and unpushed; findings update #FQ5007 and #3UTRKA; the completion
note names #FTZ8FD without beginning it.

** TODO Materialize a demand-streamed authored world
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: H1ASQU
:END:

/Intent./ Replace the eager whole-scene store for ordinary play with one
deterministic Luft source, immutable resident chunks, sparse authored overlay,
camera demand, worker production, incarnation validation, and bounded
eviction.  Compose an authored river, road, pass, and one masonry destination
across chunk seams before adding broad procedural detail.

/Evidence./ Luvcraft proves the ownership pattern, but Luft's full-height

remain distinct.  A finite-scene adapter should preserve atelier fixtures
without making them define the new source protocol.

/Done when./ Walking across a boundary causes observable source generation,
materialization, meshing, publication, and opposite-edge eviction; resident
CPU and GPU state remain bounded; stale worker results are rejected; the
authored route and destination survive chunk-independent generation; and the
completion note names #JDB26Y.

** TODO Publish stable regional sky and RGB4 light
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: JDB26Y
:END:

/Intent./ Add separately revised sky and block-light fields, palette-projected
opacity/emission tables, explicit open/closed/unknown boundary semantics,
immutable regional captures, frontier propagation, and appearance-only
publication.

/Evidence./ The current Luft finite RGB4 solver is a differential oracle;

than generalize the executor prematurely.

/Done when./ Open ground, a roofed room, a skylight, and an RGB emitter render
correctly; one source and receiver cross a chunk seam; entering visible chunks
wait for stable guard data; relighting preserves geometry; differential tests
agree with the finite oracle; and the completion note names #DLH49J.

** TODO Make the first journey worth taking
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: DLH49J
:END:

/Intent./ Extend the proved source into a coherent first region with connected
and branching routes, named destinations, wilderness between them, durable
edits, and a resumable player position.  Tune generation and residency from
measured walking rather than from fixture construction time.

/Evidence./ Architectural composition is the product requirement that keeps a
large generated domain from becoming an oscillating height-map no-place.
Persistence should save source description, semantic edits, and resume state,
not resident chunks, light, meshes, or GPU products.

/Done when./ A player can leave the initial fortress, follow and lose a road,
cross at least one distant authored structure, return by another route, edit a
cell, restart, and find both edit and position preserved while residency stays
bounded.  The worksheet then decides the next measured need rather than
assuming transparency, LoD, vertical streaming, or simulation.

* Decision log
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: 3UTRKA
:END:

** 2026-08-28 — Preserve geometry and add a parallel appearance product

The simplified four-word star stream and fixed one-width atlas remain the
entire terrain geometry contract.  Material identity and voxel light travel in
a one-for-one active-star sidecar interpreted by static atlas selectors.  Do
not restore face stocks, material-dependent topology, mixed widths, repair
passes, or one draw/atlas per material.

** 2026-08-28 — Reuse substrate laws, not Luvcraft world classes

Finite domains, vocabularies, columnar materializations, immutable worker
captures, demand tokens, sparse overlays, derived-field revisions, explicit
boundary semantics, and coherent publication are valuable below either game.
Luvcraft's coordinate axes, 16-cubed storage, =block-kind= semantics, and
gameplay mutation contracts are not Luft's model.  Luft becomes the second
client whose concrete needs can justify later frontier-executor extraction.

** 2026-08-28 — Authored macrostructure precedes procedural detail

The large world begins with routes, watersheds, regions, sightlines, and sparse
destinations expressed in absolute world coordinates.  Deterministic noise and
local rules fill and roughen that composition; they do not substitute for it.

** 2026-08-28 — Stable visible light uses known guard content

Nonresidency remains unknown.  The playable path retains or withholds a visible
product until the content required to settle its bounded sky and RGB4 light is
known.  Provisional fields may aid inspection but are not presented as final
geography.
