#+startup: overview

* Sixteen local views of three cells
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: G4HUOG
:END:

A small occupied region does not become geometry all at once.  This little
L-shape touches sixteen lattice sites.  Each site sees its own eight-cell star
and contributes only the triangles it owns.

#+begin_src lisp-figure
(region-plate '((0 0 0) (1 0 0) (0 1 0)))
#+end_src

The middle view explodes the sixteen contributions away from their lattice
sites.  Color identifies ownership, not material.  Returning those pieces to
their global coordinates produces the right-hand mesh without welding,
deduplication, or a second geometric interpretation: it is the concatenation
of the production =star-atlas-owned-triangles= lookups.

The figure is itself the value of a Lisp expression in this Org page.  Its
=region-plate= CLOS object retains the occupied cell coordinates; the Typst
and web renderers ask the object for one deterministic Typst fragment.  Thus
the PDF and SVG are two presentations of the same production-derived object,
not separately maintained illustrations.
