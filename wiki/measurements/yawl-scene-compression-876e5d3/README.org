* Result: fewer triangles, not a demonstrated GPU gain

At native 1920x1080, live alpine's nine scene-visible residents contain:

| Product                   | Original | Compressed |
|---------------------------+----------+------------|
| Scene triangles           |  1925590 |    1123274 |
| Scene draws               |     1163 |          9 |
| Referenced vertex records |  2849173 |    2127698 |

The final row counts CPU vertex-reference records, not GPU invocations.
The compressed reference/index buffers add 30,500,872 bytes (29.09 MiB);
the original resident and atlas buffers remain allocated. Straight-line
boundary simplification removes 41.7% of scene triangles. An earlier probe
that retained every boundary vertex removed only 8.4%.

All means below are milliseconds. Each cell lists independent runs, not
percentiles. Three-scene runs use alpine, cave, then live alpine; the other
runs measure only live alpine.

| Live alpine                 | CPU encode            | GPU execution         | Serialized completion |
|-----------------------------+-----------------------+-----------------------+-----------------------|
| Original (control-1/2)      | 2.280 / 2.097 / 2.201 | 13.762/13.044/14.234  | 16.692/15.792/17.098  |
| Batch only (batch-1)        | 1.747 / 1.571         | 13.709/13.619         | 16.139/15.850         |
| Compressed (coordinated-1/2)| 1.650 / 1.487 / 1.613 | 13.755/13.526/13.763  | 16.092/15.706/16.064  |
| Flat + compressed (flat-1)  | 3.274 / 3.045         | 11.811/11.430         | 15.882/15.209         |

Compression does not establish a GPU win over batching. The final code's
separate preparation census takes 0.80--0.99 seconds per visible live-alpine
resident, on the render owner in this probe. This intentionally simple
list/hash/ear-clipping implementation is unsuitable for live streaming.

The flat experiment removes *all* bevels, including those near the camera;
it is not distance LoD or a transition implementation. It reduces the nine
residents to 157,694 triangles and lowers GPU cost, but also selects the
existing unmerged flat-shadow path. Its increased CPU cost includes that
shadow-path change. Do not attribute the entire difference to scene LoD.

Alpine's triangles fall 63,754 -> 34,868; cave's 101,820 -> 51,012.
Their single control/treatment runs are insufficient for a timing claim.

* What the prototype preserves

=batch.lisp= adapts the earlier exact scene-batching probe. Each vertex
references its original site and atlas vertex. The original material,
light, motion, and fragment computations remain in use. Shadows delegate
to the existing renderer. Only visible residents receive probe buffers;
buffers survive capture and host teardown before release.

=compress.lisp= groups by oriented integer plane, selected material-code
lanes, occupied/empty mask counts, and constant packed voxel light. One
nonzero material contribution may move between otherwise zero lanes;
multiple contributions keep their lane order. Nonconstant-light triangles
stay intact. This is stricter than equality of final material outputs.

Groups with holes or ambiguous boundaries fall back to original triangles.
Candidate groups remove interior vertices. A boundary vertex may disappear
only when it is collinear on all candidate boundaries meeting there and is
not used by a retained triangle. Unpaired edges retain both endpoints:
residents never independently simplify shared chunk seams. The simple
polygon boundaries are then ear-clipped. The output compacts unused vertex
references. No semantic mesh, appearance sidecar, or light field is changed.

The final incidence guard and added count logging postdate the timed runs.
The final preparation run produced identical triangle counts for all eleven
measured scene residents. The guard rejects edges incident to more than two
triangles rather than allowing opposite directed edges to mask them.

* Validation and limits

=check.lisp= performs 98 CPU differential comparisons: all 256 stars, an
asymmetric cuboid, tunnel, hollow shell, and seeded random occupancy, with
uniform/mixed materials, constant/varying light, and bevelled/flat geometry.
The oracle compares oriented unit-tick boundary segments per material,
plane and constant light; nonconstant-light triangles must remain intact.
It separately compares unsplit mesh boundary edges, detecting new
T-junctions or changed resident-edge segmentation. Counts never increase.

Metal compiled and rendered alpine, cave and live alpine. The three final
captures were inspected: no obvious new holes or cracks. The alpine recess
and foreground green/gray cliff stripes also occur in the control.
Captures are 3840x2160 even though timed targets are 1920x1080. They are
*not pixel-identical*: =image-diff.json= excludes the changing HUD at y<64.
Live alpine has a mean absolute channel difference of 0.432/255, maximum
55/255; 83 pixels differ by more than 8/255. Do not call this a proven
bit-exact image replacement. No Vulkan runtime or moving-camera LoD test
was performed; this prototype introduces no LoD selection.

Original captures, review thumbnails, logs, and the earlier all-boundary
probe remain in the thread's =.amp/in/artifacts/scene-merge/= directory.
The files here retain the final runnable experiment and raw timing data.

* Reproduce in a disposable process

From the checkout root, after =make=:

#+begin_src sh
BENCH_LIBRARY=1 BENCH_MODE=native-1x BENCH_SIZES=1920x1080 \
BENCH_SCENES=live-alpine BENCH_REPEATS=2 BENCH_WARMUP=120 BENCH_FRAMES=600 \
SCENE_MERGE=1 BENCH_OUTPUT=build/scene-compression.sexp \
./env sbcl --script wiki/measurements/yawl-scene-compression-876e5d3/benchmark.lisp

BENCH_LIBRARY=1 ./env sbcl --script \
  wiki/measurements/yawl-scene-compression-876e5d3/check.lisp
#+end_src

=SCENE_MERGE=0= selects the original renderer; =batch= selects only batching.
=SCENE_FLAT=1= additionally removes all bevels. Unset it for exact-surface
comparisons. =BENCH_CAPTURE_DIR= optionally saves images after timing.
Never load these method/function replacements into a user's live Sly image.

Runs use 120 warmup / 600 timed frames, no profiler, no concurrent builds.
Measurements are serialized offscreen completion, not display FPS.
Clock frequencies and thermal state are not locked. Runs are sequential,
not randomized: control-1, coordinated-1, control-2, flat-1, coordinated-2,
batch-1, with other exploratory runs between some cohorts. Small differences
are inconclusive. =preparation.txt= is a separate one-frame census, not a
frame-timing sample. Retained output may contain skipped-old metadata forms;
only forms with =:BACKEND :LUFT= are measurements.
