#+title: Compact visibility-first terrain, with invariant positions

Measured on yawl, Apple M5, macOS 27.2, 2026-10-25. Production changes follow
=c8f1d28=. Campaign: #EAWKSG. This replaces neither the terrain topology nor
the bevel atlas. Merging, analytic proxy polygons and approximate appearance
retirement remain experiments.

* The production representation keeps the original triangles

Visibility pulls the original shared atlas indices and resident sites. Two
RGBA8 targets retain the resident-local site and a packed resident/atlas
triangle identity. A fullscreen material pass rejects other residents before
reading their buffers, reconstructs the winning triangle's plane and voxel
light, and evaluates the existing material and lighting formulas. It retains
the visibility pass's hardware depth. All terrain material draws precede
fixtures so unconditional material writes cannot paint over another resident's
crate. Existing publication and target-generation ownership retires bindings
on edits and resize.

There is no expanded per-corner mesh, CPU merger or new resident geometry
upload. The new numeric payload is eight bytes per internal pixel: 16,588,800
bytes at native 1920x1080, or 4,718,592 bytes at the measured default internal
1024x576. This excludes GPU alignment and program/binding objects. Original
resident preparation and upload are unchanged; measured clone/setup time
includes a whole second renderer, not the incremental feature cost.

The material pass computes world gradients from four samples on the same
triangle plane. Only three height evaluations are needed: the current sample
and its horizontal and vertical partners. No neighboring surface contributes
a gradient. Original material hooks, rounded-edge formulas, shadows and
unjittered motion clips remain in use. This is more expensive per surviving
pixel than forward shading; savings come from avoiding hidden and partial-
facet material invocations. Color is not bit-identical to forward hardware
interpolation and implementation-selected derivatives.

* Final Metal timings retain the gain in both orders

Seed-7 live alpine/meadow, settled nine source chunks / sixteen renderer
residents, camera (1009,1000,45), yaw .9, pitch -.55, FOV .85, exposure .45,
frozen sky clock. Bevels are explicitly passed to the host: changing the
renderer special alone does not override =play='s plain-face default.

Each final cohort warms 80 adjacent A/B frames and records 400 timed frames,
200 per path, in ABBA or BAAB order. Timing is full GPU command-buffer work,
including shadow, scene, reconstruction and presentation; readbacks are
outside the timed loop. Dynamic resolution is disabled. These are serialized
offscreen GPU times, not displayed FPS or independent candidate-only clocks.

Median within-block ratios: about 25–27% less GPU time natively and 44–47%
less with normal temporal settings at 1920x1080 output. Default rendering
keeps the existing output-pixel budget, so its internal size is NOT 1440x810.
Forward/native medians range 15.37–16.76 ms; visibility 11.08–12.42 ms.
Default forward medians range 14.11–14.86 ms; visibility 7.65–7.90 ms.
CPU encoding is not the claimed gain and varies with the governor/workload.

Plain-face live terrain at 640x360 output / 480x270 internal also improves:
ratios .7136 alpine and .7339 meadow. A small flat cave loses .27 ms
(2.1275 to 2.4007 ms, ratio 1.1273). The forward path remains available with
=luft.render::*terrain-visibility-p*= NIL. No unmeasured density heuristic
silently changes representation. MSAA retains the original forward path;
its cave and balls control images match exactly.

* Position invariance closes a real depth failure

Before invariance, grazing alpine after resident removal chose a surface
26.608 cells away at one sample (x249,y252 of 480x270). Other motion samples
had small depth ULP differences despite identical geometry. Both vertex
shaders computed the same mathematical position, but independent compiler
optimization was not required to produce the same raster coordinates.

Both terrain position outputs now request =:invariant t=. Metal emits
=[[position, invariant]]= and enables =preserveInvariance=; the option alone
is insufficient. SPIR-V, WGSL and HLSL also lower the explicit contract.
Unannotated shaders retain their previous compilation semantics. See
[[https://developer.apple.com/metal/Metal-Shading-Language-Specification.pdf][Apple's MSL specification]] and
[[https://developer.apple.com/documentation/metal/mtlcompileoptions/preserveinvariance][preserveInvariance]].

Final Metal acceptance covers 321 paired frames and 51,503,616 depth samples:
zero depth-byte differences, zero coverage differences and zero nonfinite
depth/HDR/motion samples. It includes 192 grazing poses, 96 plain-face motion
poses, resident removal/republication and target resizing. The exact failing
removal case now matches bit-for-bit. Current-frame motion remains close,
not bit-identical, because its interpolation is reconstructed.

Beveled native RGB HDR mean absolute error is .002718 alpine / .002054 meadow;
default temporal input .001012 / .000252. Grazing-path RGB error is about
.0000125 / .00000632. Native presentation differs by at most 5/9 channel
levels out of 255; default alpine reaches 14. =grazing-comparison.mp4= is
inspected 12-fps comparison playback, not a throughput demonstration.
The blue-gray cliff bands and small detached voxel details in the stills
also occur in the exact-depth forward control.

A pre-invariance MetalFX cohort had one transient black patch in the forward
presentation only, not its finite HDR input. A forward/forward repeat control
was byte-identical over 96 poses. Its cause is unresolved; it is not evidence
of a visibility regression or a claimed fix. Raw exploratory evidence remains
under =.amp/in/artifacts/visibility-production/=.

* Vulkan stays opt-in

On chapel's RX 6600 XT / RADV, preliminary three-height plain-face tests at
640x360 showed identical depth and small color errors, but 9–14% more GPU
time. This is not evidence about beveled 1080p performance. Linux therefore
keeps forward rendering by default rather than inheriting a Mac-only win.

The final invariant Vulkan run explicitly enables bevels and candidate
construction. Its ten A/B comparisons (5,587,200 depth samples) are byte-
identical, including native1080 alpine. Every forward-repeat is also exact,
and the ordinary Linux default builds no visibility pipeline. Color is not
strictly equivalent: native1080 alpine has presentation RGB MAE .471 channel
levels, maximum 23, with 6.41% of pixels differing by more than two levels.
At 640x360 the 99th-percentile channel error is zero. The cause of the larger
native1080 shading differences remains unverified; no Vulkan performance
claim follows from this final image-validation run.

Validation completes all captures but exits 2 on pre-existing errors that
also reproduce with visibility disabled: present-timing lacks calibrated-
timestamps, and unrelated mixed blend pipelines lack independentBlend.
There is no new error category. This change does not fix those issues.

* Reproduce

Run in a disposable SBCL process, not by loading =run.lisp= into a live game.
The harness is Metal-specific; the ordinary production path is portable.

#+begin_src sh
BENCH_LIBRARY=1 BENCH_MODE=default BENCH_SIZES=1920x1080 \
BENCH_SCENES=live-alpine,live-meadow BENCH_REPEATS=1 \
BENCH_WARMUP=80 BENCH_FRAMES=400 VIS_REVERSE=0 \
VIS_OUTPUT=build/visibility/release-default-0 BENCH_OUTPUT=build/visibility/run.sexp \
./env sbcl --dynamic-space-size 12000 \
  --script wiki/measurements/yawl-visibility-c8f1d28/run.lisp

uv run --no-project --with numpy python \
  wiki/measurements/yawl-visibility-c8f1d28/analyze.py build/visibility

make test
make
#+end_src

Repeat with =VIS_REVERSE=1= and a fresh output folder, then with
=BENCH_MODE=native-1x=. =VIS_BEVELS=0= selects plain faces. =VIS_MOTION=96=
records the moving camera; =VIS_GRAZE=1= selects the horizon-crossing path.
=VIS_EDIT=1= removes and republishes a resident; =VIS_RESIZE=1= grows then
restores the target. =VIS_NULL=1= runs forward in both renderers, each retaining
its own history. =VIS_SHADER=1= forces the portable temporal reconstruction.
Warmup and timed counts must be divisible by four.

All 35 test suites and both application builds pass. The actual durable game
was reloaded, verified with visibility and bevels active, screenshot-inspected,
and left playing with a healthy canvas loop. Raw accepted timing tables and
analysis accompany this bundle; large readbacks stay locally excluded.
