#+title: Visibility-first shading wins on real beveled terrain

Apple M5 / Metal 4, macOS 27.2, 2026-10-25. Base revision
[[https://github.com/mbrock/luv/commit/7e857b770c732fd097c532aabee56c437b46caa4][7e857b7]]; =source-hashes.json= pins the final experimental sources.
Thread: https://ampcode.com/threads/T-01a1725e-3226-740f-a884-eaa984f5905a
Campaign: #EKK3FS; follow-up: #EAWKSG. Production defaults are unchanged.

* Keep visibility-first shading; do not require geometric merging

The earlier bounded analytic proxy loses to triangulated patches (#ETRAG8).
This experiment tests a different execution path on actual generated terrain:
rasterize exact triangles into a cheap triangle-ID buffer with hardware depth,
then reconstruct the winning triangle's material inputs and shade its pixels.
The expensive production material no longer runs on every tiny triangle's
partially covered fragment quads. All triangle processing still happens; this
is not a software rasterizer, hierarchical LOD, or integrated pixel footprint.

Native 1920x1080 GPU milliseconds, means from 100 samples per variant per
world/order. Each cell is initial/reversed variant order. Complete frames
include the unchanged shadow pass, sky, scene extras and presentation pass.

| Variant                         | Live alpine     | Live meadow     |
|---------------------------------+-----------------+-----------------|
| Production packed shared atlas  | 15.982 / 15.570 | 15.992 / 17.097 |
| Predecoded indexed mesh         | 12.971 / 12.613 | 13.211 / 14.133 |
| Predecoded merged mesh          | 13.243 / 12.884 | 12.893 / 13.816 |
| Exact merged, production decode | 14.221 / 13.968 | 13.518 / 14.464 |
| Visibility-first, original mesh |  8.864 /  8.767 |  9.396 /  9.903 |
| Visibility-first, merged mesh   |  8.008 /  7.858 |  8.421 /  8.791 |

Mean within-cycle GPU ratios are 0.555/0.565 alpine and 0.589/0.591 meadow
for original-mesh visibility, and 0.501/0.506 and 0.527/0.524 for merged
visibility. Thus most of the gain does not require changed triangulation.
The simpler predecoded control also helps, but does not explain away the
visibility-first advantage. Merging the predecoded alpine control actually
slows it slightly: fewer triangles are not sufficient evidence of a win.

Frequency varies during these runs. Adjacent symmetric cycles and reversed
order make the ratios more useful than isolated absolute times. These are
serialized offscreen measurements, NOT displayed FPS or fixed-clock costs.
No profiler or other GPU test ran concurrently. No new Xcode shader profile,
helper invocation counters, or GPU-unit attribution was collected: the causal
description above is the algorithm, not a measured breakdown of its gain.

* Original-mesh visibility preserves depth; merging has rare failures

At both static 1080p views, original-mesh visibility has bit-identical depth
and coverage to production. Scene-linear RGB MAE is 0.002817 alpine and
0.002111 meadow; shading is not identical. Reconstructed interpolation and
derivatives over neighboring surfaces differ from triangle-local derivatives.
Do not interpret mean error as a bound on every pixel.

Two 96-pose camera flights at 640x360 cover 27,202,799 reference-covered
samples. All variants receive identical camera uniforms at every pose.

| Variant                    | Coverage differences | Common-hit depth errors >1 cell |
|----------------------------+----------------------+---------------------------------|
| Visibility, original mesh  |                    0 |                               0 |
| Visibility, merged mesh    |                    1 |                              41 |
| Merged + appearance, 3 px  |                    1 |                              41 |

Original-mesh depth is bit-identical throughout both flights. The merged
maximum view-depth disagreement is 10.02 cells alpine / 13.09 cells meadow.
These are hundreds to thousands of depth-buffer ULPs, not just inverse-depth
quantization. Integer surface/boundary checks establish the CPU merge's
geometric contract, but do not establish identical GPU raster edge ownership.
The precise GPU cause of these rare wrong-depth samples remains unresolved.
The merger must not be called raster-exact or accepted silently.

Across the motion frames, mean scene-linear RGB MAE is 0.00000837/0.00000614
for original-mesh visibility and 0.00001020/0.00000763 for merged visibility.
These differ from the 1080p errors: resolution changes procedural filtering.
Residual changes between adjacent frames are recorded, but are not an
antialiasing metric or a claim of eliminating temporal shimmer.

Both four-way videos and the 1080p candidate/control stills were inspected.
No large candidate-only hole, terrain disappearance or switching pop was
visible. Blue-gray recessed cliff bands and sand/grass discontinuities also
exist in the matched original controls. The rare depth failures are retained
numerically even though they are not apparent in the overview videos.

=live-alpine-motion.mp4= and =live-meadow-motion.mp4= show production at
top-left, original-mesh visibility at top-right, merged visibility bottom-left,
and merged visibility plus 3-pixel appearance retirement bottom-right.
Playback is deliberately 12 fps for inspection, not real-time throughput.

* Appearance retirement is not yet a clear winner

The appearance-only candidate retains bevel geometry/normals and the real
lighting, roughness/specular, material character, blocklight, shadow and
atmosphere hooks. It substitutes approximate unresolved material detail below
a projected bevel-radius threshold, blends to production by twice that size,
and does no normal or front/back depth averaging. The threshold is in actual
render-target pixels, not output-window pixels. These approximations are not
energy-preserving integrals. Optional world-anchored pigment exists in the
generator but was not enabled in these GPU comparisons.

At 1280x720, limit 1 increases atlas GPU time from 16.068 to 17.303 ms alpine
and 13.220 to 15.545 ms meadow. It also increases merged visibility time from
6.539 to 6.685 and 5.836 to 6.003 ms. The independently rebuilt unmodified
shader control is pixel-identical to production in both worlds.

At 640x360, limit 3 gives small exploratory savings over merged visibility:
2.834 to 2.666 ms alpine, 2.676 to 2.625 ms meadow. This threshold has no
reversed-order confirmation. Its motion RGB MAE rises to 0.0000762/0.0000487.
Near-path derivatives remain inside dynamically selected branches; quads at
retirement boundaries are not portable without an explicit footprint design.
Neither this branch nor the pigment option is ready for production.

* Representation and preparation costs remain substantial

All geometry uses the production bevel-T atlas and fixed eighth-cell bevels.
Merging retains ordered material lanes, mask counts, packed light, normals,
normal-selected site-W edge bytes and resident seams. Varying-light triangles
retain their original references; hole/ambiguous groups fall back. Shadows
keep the production representation. Scene residency and culling are unchanged.

| Resident geometry                | Alpine    | Meadow    |
|----------------------------------+-----------+-----------|
| Renderer residents               |        16 |        16 |
| Source triangles                 | 3,375,886 | 2,584,040 |
| Merged triangles                 | 1,943,002 | 1,261,114 |
| Source sites/appearance/light MB  |      8.58 |      6.17 |
| Expanded visibility payload MB   |     89.72 |     68.31 |
| Merged visibility payload MB     |     55.33 |     36.56 |
| Predecoded indexed payload MB    |     83.19 |     57.68 |
| Predecoded merged payload MB     |     53.15 |     30.54 |
| CPU expand + merge seconds       |     14.56 |     12.69 |
| Additional predecode seconds     |      7.00 |      4.68 |

Decimal MB, numeric payloads rather than heap high-water marks. Visibility
totals include the shared vertex/normal atlas; they exclude common descriptor,
camera, shadow and target resources. At 1080p the extra RGBA8 ID target is
8,294,400 bytes. Direct forward variants additionally need their u32 index
streams. The harness retains multiple candidates and original resources
simultaneously; the table is per-representation, not total process memory.
The 16 renderer residents differ from the benchmark's 9 loaded source chunks.
Triangle counts include all residents, not only this camera's submitted ones.

The CPU implementation is intentionally allocation-heavy and immutable.
Uploads, program construction, edits and publication are excluded from frame
timings. No streaming/edit throughput claim is warranted. A useful next step
is compact primitive identification against the shared atlas, not shipping
these expanded references or the current multi-second merger.

* Reproduce the matched-world experiment

Use a disposable SBCL process, never load =run.lisp= into the managed game.
The harness uses =scripts/bench-gpu-fps.lisp='s seed-7 live alpine/meadow
worlds, camera (1009,1000,45), yaw .9, pitch -.55, field of view .85, fixed
exposure .45 and frozen sky clock. Residency settles before measurement.
Temporal reconstruction, MSAA and dynamic resolution are off. Timed frames
have no readbacks. All shader/resource construction precedes warmup.

The host =play= function defaults its explicit bevel argument to NIL, even
when the renderer special is T. An initial live cohort therefore had a flat
control and was rejected. =run.lisp= now explicitly passes T and asserts the
realized renderer option. No rejected-cohort numbers appear in this result.

#+begin_src sh
BENCH_LIBRARY=1 BENCH_MODE=native-1x BENCH_SIZES=1920x1080 \
BENCH_SCENES=live-alpine,live-meadow BENCH_REPEATS=1 \
BENCH_WARMUP=120 BENCH_FRAMES=600 \
LOD_MODES=control,baked,baked-merged,merged,visibility,visibility-merged \
LOD_OUTPUT=build/terrain-lod/focused BENCH_OUTPUT=build/terrain-lod/focused.sexp \
./env sbcl --dynamic-space-size 12000 \
  --script wiki/measurements/yawl-terrain-lod-7e857b7/run.lisp

uv run --no-project --with numpy \
  python wiki/measurements/yawl-terrain-lod-7e857b7/analyze.py build/terrain-lod/focused

./env sbcl --script wiki/measurements/yawl-terrain-lod-7e857b7/check.lisp
make test
#+end_src

For the reversed cohort, reverse =LOD_MODES= and use a fresh output root.
For 720p exploratory appearance, use 96 warmup / 480 timed frames and modes
=control,copy,direct,merged,visibility,visibility-merged,atlas-lod,visibility-merged-lod=.
For motion, use 640x360, 48 warmup / 240 timed frames, =LOD_LIMIT=3=,
=LOD_MOTION=96=, and =control,visibility,visibility-merged,visibility-merged-lod=.
Warmup and timed counts must be divisible by twice the mode count.
The motion path travels 24 cells, rises 65, and turns sinusoidally; it does
not update residency, simulate the world, or measure streaming.

Evidence folders retain raw timings, geometry/baked statistics, benchmark
metadata and full analysis. Large readbacks remain locally excluded under
=.amp/in/artifacts/terrain-lod/=. Inspection videos and selected native stills
are retained here. =check.lisp= passes 140 geometry comparisons and 6,656
edge-byte checks with deliberate broken-merge controls. =make test= passes
all 35 suites including strict Parinfer. Final resource-table cleanup was
rerun on GPU; control/baked/baked-merged HDR and depth match the earlier
smoke images bit-for-bit. No production renderer or DSL change was needed.
