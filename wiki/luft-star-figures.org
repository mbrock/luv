A Luft star is both a neighborhood predicate and a small piece of geometry.
The four views below follow the exact =#x1B= production star without changing
projection or color vocabulary: occupied incident cells are rose, ordinary
faces are blue, connecting bands are amber, and the triangles at the central
junction are red.

The first view is the cubical input.  The second is the whole local atlas
patch.  The third removes the color fields and exposes the quad and triangle
structure, including the diagonals by which quads become GPU triangles.  The
fourth keeps the whole patch as registration context but emphasizes only the
polygons owned by this lattice vertex.

This is deliberately not a pipeline diagram.  It is one mathematical object
held under four lenses, so correspondences can be checked rather than merely
asserted.  The polygon coordinates are generated from =star-triangles= and
=star-atlas-owned-triangles=, the same definitions used by Luft itself.
